The FEQ (Festival d'Été de Québec) schedule is out, but the experience for comparing evenings and finding artists you want to see on the website is not very good. This is a project to create a very simple, barebones table UI in front of the site's data.

# Problems to Solve

## Reduce lost space

I should be able to see much more data at once than 3 days and one or two shows, especially on a desktop computer.

## Better filtering

It should be easy to hide paid/free shows. The main site hides smaller local artists at the bottom, multiple screen distances away.

## Clearer venues

Focus on venues locations more than their sponsor name. There are two scenes that are at the same location, alternating between the two, but are shown as two completely different schedules on the site. It's the same event at the same location, just left or right.

## Easy to print

That one guy that had my schedule spreadsheet on his fridge should be able to do that this year.

## Hard to see artist details

Navigating to artist resets context when you go back. It's a mess. I would just make a popup box for it.
